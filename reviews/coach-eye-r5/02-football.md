## What is right (praise)

- **His skeleton, slot for slot, now with slot 5.** A = two-arm row, two-leg squat, vertical pull, single-leg knee lift, **biceps**, leg extension. B = horizontal press, two-leg hinge, incline / vertical press, single-leg hinge, **triceps**, leg curl (Nordic on Tuesday). The athletic block comes first, then power, then strength. Knee work is paired with pulls and hip work with pushes. Horizontal comes before vertical on every day. Every day holds 9-12 exercises plus the 2 core exercises, inside the field-athlete cap, and the header says so truthfully.
- **Core first, power before strength.** The core block opens every day. The Olympic lifts sit on the right days: cleans on the squat days, snatches on the hinge days, 8-12 sets a week, "move it fast, no percentages".
- **Spine loading is correct.** One heavy squat (Monday front squat) and one heavy hinge (Friday RDL), on different days. Thursday has a leg press, Tuesday a hyperextension.
- **The stairway is clean.** Quads and hips go 14 → 18; back, chest and shoulders 10 → 13. The final sets arrive in week 11 and hold in week 12. No step is over about 11 %, so no 15 % note is needed. The floor of 10 holds on every trained group from week 1, and no group is near 35. Set lines say exactly what moved ("+1 set on RDL (Friday): 2 → 3").
- **Progression is a rule the athlete applies.** "When you hit 10 on every set with 3 left in the tank, add weight next time and start again at 8." Effort drifts from 3 RIR to 1 RIR + a MYO set in week 12, never to failure on a compound.
- **The athletic dosing fits a linebacker.** Acceleration and falling starts; Receive-Create Land → Land & Go → Repeat; route-named cuts; the new lateral work (right-angle and lateral hurdle hops, lateral box step-up jump, lateral ankle hops), which closes r4 m12. Medicine balls stay under 20 lb. The sprint buy-out goes 1 × 20 → 2 × 15 → 3 × 10 cal. There are three aerobic sessions (Orange, Green, Blue), with the Blue intervals on Saturday, on their own.
- **Block changes are one-detail variations** on almost every lift: a pause, a squeeze, a grip, seated against standing.
- **Tests only in weeks 1 and 12.** The tested lift goes first and replaces the top set. Speed, agility and jumps are tested on Thursday.
- **Words fixed since r4:** the header count is real ("about 9-12"), "11 = your hardest week", and there is no team-practice line for an athlete with 0 practices (CH5-01). The liability line is at the top and the bottom. Each exercise has a YouTube search link and a name to copy, and every swap line says what it keeps and what it loses.
