# Coach-eye review, round 5: sample 01 (swimming, 16-year-old, middle distance, 3 dryland days)

**Grade: B+.** Lalo could hand this to the swimmer after small tweaks. Both round-4 majors are fixed in the training content. Slot 5 is back: a spider curl on the A day and a Tate press, then an overhead extension, on the B day, both printed before the leg extension and the leg curl. The front squat now climbs 2 → 3 → 4. The optional day's big-muscle compounds are gone (the FFE split squat the day after Friday's, and the extra seated row).

The one remaining major is a copy error, not a training error. The rest is minors: the hinge staircase, 1-set stations, a test-week intensifier, reps that stay high in block 3, and a few swimmer-specific picks.

## What he would like

- **His template, slot for slot.** A day: Seal Row (horizontal two-arm pull) → Front Squat (two-leg knee) → Pull-up (vertical pull) → Deficit Reverse Lunge / FFE Split Squat (single-leg knee) → Spider Curl (biceps) → Leg Extension, then calves and the face pull. B day: Flat DB Bench (horizontal push) → 45° Hyperextension (two-leg hip) → Incline DB Bench (the incline in the vertical slot, R4-LIB-03) → staggered-stance DB deadlift (single-leg hip) → Tate Press (triceps) → leg curl, then rear-delt fly, calves and a lateral raise (his athletic sheet's own 'ABdution' place). The athletic block comes first every day. Every day holds 10-12 exercises plus the 2 core exercises and the joint-care drill.
- **Order and pairing.** Core first (light, 2 × 2), then jumps → ankle hops → throws → Olympic lift → strength. Horizontal before vertical on every day. The supersets pair knee + pull (Seal Row + Front Squat, Pull-up + lunge, SA Row + KB squat) and hip + push (Flat DB Bench + hyperextension, Incline + staggered deadlift, Machine Bench + Hex).
- **Spine days.** The heavy squat is Monday and the heavy hinge is Friday. The Wednesday hinge is a non-axial hyperextension.
- **Swimming dryland (R3-ATH-01).** 3 gym days + 1 optional. No extra cardio (the pool is the cardio). Shoulder care every session, with no bands. Clean on the squat day and snatch work on the hinge day (R2-ATH-04). 36-44 throws (profile 35-45). Medballs within the balls he owns. No sprints or cuts. The 4-hour gap, the 2-week taper and the Christmas-week note are all present. CH5-01 is applied: the plan does not count the 6 practices a week and tells the athlete to adjust the load around them.
- **Stairway.** Every group reaches its final sets in week 11 and holds in the test week. The biggest weekly step is about 11 % (no 15 % note needed). Totals run 64 → 86, with no group near 35. Effort drifts 3 → 2 → 1-2 → 1 + MYO, and the MYO set lands only on machine or isolation work.
- **Progression as a rule.** For example: "when you hit 10 on every set with 3 left in the tank, add weight next time and start again at 8". The next-block rule and the editable-template lines are present (R4-PRG-03/04, CX-02..04).
- **Block changes are one-detail variations.** Examples: seated → lying leg curl, the alternating → kickstand staggered deadlift, a 1-second squeeze or pause on the rows, presses, curls and KB squat, the medball and ankle-hop progressions.
- **Liability.** The guardian release is at the top and the bottom (R4-SCP-01/02). There is no max test from zero in week 1 (R2-PER-07). Swaps say what they keep and lose. Every exercise has a YouTube search link and a name to copy (R4-LIB-04/05). The swimmer add-ons stay optional (R4-LIB-01).
