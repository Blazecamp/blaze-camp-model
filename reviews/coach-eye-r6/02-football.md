## What is right (praise)

- **His skeleton, slot for slot.** A = two-arm row, two-leg squat, vertical pull, single-leg knee lift, biceps, leg extension. B = horizontal press, two-leg hinge, incline / vertical press, single-leg hinge, triceps, leg curl (Nordic on Tuesday). The athletic block comes first, then power, then strength. Knee work is paired with pulls and hip work with pushes, horizontal before vertical. There are 9-12 exercises a day plus 2 core, inside the field-athlete cap, and the header says so.
- **The arm slot is now worded truthfully** (CH5-02, G-202): one exercise of their own, held at 2 sets, progressed with weight and reps. The tables show "7 (4 direct)" and "held: 4 direct sets a week".
- **Spine loading is correct.** One heavy squat (Monday front squat) and one heavy hinge (Friday RDL), on different days; the leg press on Thursday and a hyperextension on Tuesday.
- **The stairway is clean.** Quads and hips 14 → 18; back, chest and shoulders 10 → 13. Totals 72 → 87, with the largest weekly rise 4.8 %. Every group arrives in week 11 and holds in week 12. The floor of 10 holds from week 1 and no group comes near 35. Set lines say exactly what moved.
- **The RDL is now protected inside its own day** (G-205): no later hip lift on Friday passes it.
- **Progression is a rule the athlete applies,** and effort drifts 3 → 2 → 1-2 → 1 RIR + MYO, never to failure on a compound.
- **The athletic dosing fits a linebacker.** Accelerations and falling starts, Receive-Create Land → Land & Go → Repeat, route-named cuts, lateral hurdle and ankle hops, medicine balls of 20 lb or less, Olympic sets 8-12 a week, and 81-99 cuts against an 80-100 band.
- **Tests only in weeks 1 and 12.** The tested lift goes first and replaces its top set. Speed, agility and jump tests are on Thursday, where the falling starts and depth drops step aside.
- **Words are right:** no team-practice line for 0 practices (CH5-01), the liability line top and bottom, YouTube search links with a name to copy, and every swap says what it keeps and loses.
