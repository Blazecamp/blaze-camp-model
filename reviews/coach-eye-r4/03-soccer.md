# Coach-eye review, round 4: 03 Soccer (semi-pro midfielder, 3 days, 8 weeks, ES)

**Grade: B**: a few real fixes. I would not hand it over yet. The skeleton, order, spine days, Olympic logic, athletic doses, progression wording and liability line are right. Three things are off: Tuesday and Thursday (his "sport or off" days) become extra cardio, his two team practices are never mentioned, and Thursday is a hard-interval day before Friday's top-speed sprints. A soccer midfielder ends the block on 10 sets of flat pressing a week while his front squat stays at 3 sets. The jump menu is almost all two-foot jumps, for a cutting sport whose profile asks for 60 % single-leg.

## What is right (praise)

- **His skeleton, slot for slot.** Monday A: two-arm seal row → front squat → pull-up → step-up → leg extension → calves. Wednesday B: flat DB press → 45° hip extension (his "hip extension" option, chosen so it does not load the spine) → incline press (incline first, R4-LIB-03) → staggered single-leg deadlift → Nordic as the leg-curl slot. Friday follows his 3-day builder's day 3 (hinge + push, then knee + pull). The athletic block comes first, in his order: speed, jumps, throw, COD, Olympic.
- **Order and pairing:** core first every day, power before strength, horizontal before vertical, knee with pull and hip with push. Checks V11 / V12 / H03-H06 pass, and they hold to the eye too.
- **Spine days:** the heavy squat is on Monday and the heavy hinge on Friday. Wednesday's hinge doesn't load the spine.
- **Olympic logic is very much his:** the clean is on the squat day and the snatch on the hinge day (R2-ATH-04). The DB snatch runs his ramp, 8/8 → 6/6 → 4/4 (R2-ATH-05). The cues are speed and RIR, not percentages (R2-GEN-06).
- **Athletic doses are within the 3-day scaled profile:** 81-102 jump contacts, 87-108 cuts, 300-440 m of sprints with max velocity once a week, 20-32 rotational throws, 4-6 Olympic sets. Receive-Create stage 2 is used for an intermediate athlete, the medball is 8-10 lb, depth jumps use a low box, and the reaction drill is offered only as an add-on (R4-LIB-01).
- **Stairway:** every group reaches its final sets in week 6 (shoulders in week 5, one week early, which is allowed). Effort goes from 3 → 2 → 1-2 → 1 RIR plus a MYO set, and the MYO lands on the leg extension, exactly as his sheet preset says. The +16 % week is flagged. The pre-season is about 56 % of off-season sets, with 2 aerobic sessions (R2-PER-06, R2-ATH-06), and the plan points to the 2 taper weeks after it.
- **Progression is written as a rule the person applies** (R4-PRG-03), with the next-block rule (R4-PRG-04) and the "everything is editable" lines (CX-02 / CX-04).
- **Block-2 changes are mostly real one-detail variations:** neutral-grip DB press, one-leg leg extension, a paused side lunge, kickstand deadlift, cable row.
- **Liability:** the release line is at the top and bottom in both EN and ES. Each swap note says what it keeps and what it loses. YouTube search links and a copy-name line are on every exercise.
- No session-length fitting anywhere (CT-01). The time is shown as an estimate only.
