# Coach-eye review, round 4: sample 06, full-body aesthetics. Grade: **B**

Reviewed: `samples/plans/06-full-body-aesthetics.en.md` and `.json`, built from `samples/intakes/06-full-body-aesthetics.json` (29 years old, 3 years of training, L3, 4 days A/B plus 2 optional days, Focus glutes and side delts, full gym, 12 weeks). There is no `.es.md` for this sample, so Spanish was not graded. Session length and minutes were not judged (CT-01).

**Verdict.** The skeleton is now his. Every A day and B day matches the R4-SES-05 template slot for slot. Spinal loading, core placement, pairing, incline bias, the progression rule and the release line are all right. He would still not hand it over as it is, for three reasons:
- The volume starts where his own aesthetics track ends: 5-set first lifts and 25-28 working sets per main day from week 1.
- Because of that, the last weeks of back and chest progression live only on the optional days.
- The pyramid rep schemes on the main presses and rows are printed raw, and one of them contradicts its own note.

All three are fixable in the engine. B = a few real fixes.

## What he would like (praise)

- **His template, slot for slot.** A1/A2 run two-arm horizontal pull → two-leg squat → vertical pull → lunge → biceps → leg extension, plus calves and a rear-delt fly. B1/B2 run horizontal push → hip thrust or hex-bar deadlift → incline press → single-leg hinge → triceps → leg curl. Days hold 7-8 exercises, inside the 6-8 range. Laterality is held in every block.
- **Spinal loading done right.** Front squat (Mon) and hex-bar deadlift (Fri) are the only counted heavy squat and hinge. Thursday's squat slot is the leg press and Tuesday's hinge slot is the hip thrust.
- **Order and pairing.** Core first every day. Horizontal before vertical on every day. Knee work paired with a pull and hip work with a push in every suggested superset. Side raises open the B days for the Focus muscle, exactly his ORD-04 reading of his 4 Tracks push days.
- **Incline bias.** Four presses a week (two flat, two incline). Chest holds 19-20 on the main days without 5-set flies.
- **Rear delts and calves get their own sets** on both A days (VOL-17, R3-VOL-01). Calves get 3 sets on every A day.
- **Progression as a rule.** Every item reads "when you hit X on every set with N left in the tank, add weight and start again at Y". There are no promised weekly numbers. The stairway reaches its finals in week 11 or 12, no week jumps more than about 15 %, and effort drifts from 3 to 2 to 1-2 to 1 RIR, with a MYO set only on the last isolation exercise in week 12.
- **Product details.** The release line is at the top and the bottom. Every swap says what it keeps and loses. YouTube search links come with a name to copy. Each day lists what is easiest to remove without dropping anything. The edit and adjust guidance reads like him ("never squeeze two sessions into one").
