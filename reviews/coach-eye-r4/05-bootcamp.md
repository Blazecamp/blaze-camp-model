# Coach-eye review, round 4: sample 05, Bootcamp (3 sessions, DB + KB kit, 8 weeks)

**Grade: B**: a few real fixes before Lalo hands this out. The skeleton is his, the numbers hold, and the Spanish is good. The cardio station is wrong on two of the three days, the progression text describes a plan the client won't actually do, and shoulders end above their top number without the plan saying so.

Files reviewed: `samples/plans/05-bootcamp.en.md`, `.es.md`, `.json`; intake `samples/intakes/05-bootcamp.json` (42 y, general population, 1 year of training, Mon/Wed/Fri, FIELD_TRACK_KIT + DB (heaviest pair 35 lb) + KB, Spanish output). Template `fat_loss_bootcamp.3` (OT06 1A/2A/3A, BYOP 'BOOTCAMP - OPTION 2').
Authority used: R4 + CT-01 > CX > R3 > R2 > full > chat > sheets. Session length was not judged (CT-01). Open coach questions 8, 10, 13 and 14 in `samples/STATUS_v0.5.md` are rated minor at most.

## What Lalo would like

- **His skeleton is there.** 1A/2A/3A of BOOTCAMP - OPTION 2 are present in his order, and each session is a complete full-body dose. The cross pattern holds every day: squat + row on Monday, hinge + press on Wednesday, single-leg squat + pull on Friday (UNI-11, ORD-06).
- **7 stations every day**, inside his 5-7 (R4-SES-02, PRG-03). Timed circuits run 4-5 rounds of 35-50 s with 15 s transitions and about 90 s between rounds, exactly his bootcamp format.
- **Floor of 10 on every trained group** on a 3-day week (H17), with nothing piled on.
- **Core first**, light 2 x 2 opener. Horizontal before vertical on Wednesday (floor press before OH press).
- **Kit-honest choices.** Everything runs on a DB pair up to 35 lb, KBs, a box and medballs (10-14 lb, inside the 20-lb limit). There are no bands. The plan says up front that there is no vertical pull and what would add one, and Friday's swap prints what it keeps and loses (R4-LIB-04).
- **The progression rule itself is good.** "Pick a weight you could keep moving with about 3 reps left at the bell; add load where you finish with more left" is a rule the person applies, not a promised number (R4-PRG-03). The RIR drift is 3 → 2 → 1-2 → 1 + one intensifier (R3-VOL-04).
- **Block 2 mostly changes one detail** (goblet → high-Zercher squat, KB → DB deadlift, alternating → seesaw press, overhand → underhand rows, hammer → Zottman curl).
- **Optional add-ons** (wall sit, farmer carry dosed by distance or time, no muscle credit) are outside the template (R4-LIB-01/02). YouTube search links come with the name to copy (R4-LIB-05).
- **The liability line** prints at the top and bottom in EN and ES (R4-SCP-02).
- **The Spanish is natural**, neutral Latin American, with English gym terms in brackets throughout ("Sentadilla goblet al cajón (Box Goblet Squat)", "Remo con pecho apoyado (Chest-Supported Row)").
