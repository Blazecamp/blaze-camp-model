## What is right (praise)

- **The skeleton is his.** Non-test weeks run A = two-arm seal row, front squat, chin-up / pull-up, side lunge, leg extension. B = bench, hip extension, incline press, single-leg hinge, leg curl, side / rear-delt raise. Thursday and Friday hold the same slots with the second exposure (leg press, single-arm pulldown, Bulgarian split squat; DB bench, RDL, DB overhead press, staggered single-leg deadlift). Knee work pairs with pulls and hip work with pushes, and horizontal comes before vertical.
- **Athletic block first, power before strength, core first.** Sprint, jump, throw, cut, then the Olympic lift, then strength. It is his order, and every main day stays inside the field-athlete cap of 12.
- **Olympic lifts are dosed sensibly and placed his way.** Cleans on the squat days (power clean and jerk Monday, power clean Thursday) and snatches on the hinge days (power snatch Tuesday, snatch pull Friday). 8-12 sets a week, inside the profile range. The rule is "move it fast, no percentages".
- **Axial loading is correct.** One heavy squat (front squat, Monday) and one heavy hinge (RDL, Friday), on different days. Thursday is a leg press and Tuesday a hip extension.
- **The stairway works.** Quads and hips 14 to 18, back, chest and shoulders 10 to 13. Final sets are reached in week 11 and held in week 12. No step is over about 8 %. The floor of 10 holds on every trained group from week 1. The steps are one set at a time with honest "+1 set on X" lines, and the weeks that hold say "add a little load or reps".
- **Progression is a rule the athlete applies**: "when you hit 10 on every set with 3 left in the tank, add weight next time and start again at 8". No promised numbers. Effort drifts from 3 RIR to 1 + one MYO set in week 12, never to failure on a compound.
- **The athletic work fits the sport.** Acceleration starts and falling starts (the sport's acceleration focus), Receive-Create stages per block (land, land & go, repeat), route-named cuts tiered by turn (his ATH-06), ankle hops, medicine balls of 8-10 lb (under the 20-lb limit), and sprint buy-outs that grow from 1 x 20 to 3 x 10 calories. Three aerobic sessions a week (profile off-season 3), with the hard Blue intervals on their own day.
- **The block changes are real one-detail variations**: single-arm pulldown to the 1-second squeeze, lying to seated leg curl, a side raise to the seated version, chin-up to pull-up. The main lifts stay.
- **The tests** are in week 1 and week 12 only. In each test week the tested lift goes first and the test replaces its top set.
- **The liability line** is at the top and the bottom, there is a YouTube search link with a name to copy, and the swap lines say what each swap keeps and loses.
