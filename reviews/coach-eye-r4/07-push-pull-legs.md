## Praise

- **His template, slot for slot.** All six days follow the sheet's slot order. Push goes H-push → V-push → incline → fly → dip → 2 triceps; pull goes 2 rows → pulldown → side raise → reverse fly → 2 curls; legs go BKD → BHD → UKD → leg ext → leg curl → UHD → Focus slot. The Focus slot holds calves, which is honest because Focus is chest/back. The only deviation is Saturday's lead lift (m2).
- **Exercise count** is 7 a day plus one bonus on Monday: inside 6-8 for aesthetics (R4-SES-02), core and warm-up not counted.
- **Incline bias done right** (R4-LIB-03). The "vertical push" slot is a high-incline press on both days, not an overhead press, and each push day adds a second incline press. The plan stays heavy on the flat bench for a powerlifting-style lifter.
- **Axial loading**: heavy squat on Wednesday, heavy deadlift on Saturday. The other lower lifts are non-axial (hip thrust, leg press, single-leg DB work) (R2-SES-01/02, PER-04).
- **Core first**, 2 x 2, light, every day (R2-SES-03). **Rows before pulldowns** on both pull days (ORD-01). Arms go with their synergists, as on his sheet.
- **Focus** chest and back sit 2 sets above the balanced plan and lead their days (R3-SES-01 "an extra set or two or three"; ORD-04).
- **Stairway**: smooth 2-5% weekly rises. Every group arrives in week 11 and holds in week 12 with load/reps (R4-PRG-01, CX-02/03). Sets are added to the first exercise of the group first (VOL-07, STATUS fix 2). The effort ramp runs 3 → 2 → 1-2 → 1 RIR + MYO (R3).
- **Progression as a rule**, on every lift: "when you hit 6 on every set with 3 left in the tank, add weight next time and start again at 4" (R4-PRG-03). The next block starts about 5% higher and never above the top (R4-PRG-04). The template-is-editable lines follow CX-04.
- **Most block changes are real one-detail variations**: neutral grip, DB between the feet, pause at the bottom or top, one leg at a time, overhand grip, DB → cable fly (R2-LIB-01).
- **Swap lines say what each swap keeps and loses** (R4-LIB-04). The YouTube search link and the name to copy are printed under every exercise (R4-LIB-05).
- **Liability** is printed at the top and the bottom, with release acceptance stamped (R4-SCP-01/02). Test days are given in words, and Christmas / New Year is flagged.
- **Four aerobic colours** for a physique client (R2-SES-06), kept off the leg days, with a clear "move it to a free day" line.
