# Coach-eye review (round 4): 04 CrossFit-style class: Grade B-

Reviewed: `samples/plans/04-crossfit-style-bootcamp.en.md` and `.json` (engine 0.5.0, model 1.3.0), intake `samples/intakes/04-crossfit-style-bootcamp.json` (35 y, L2, 2 years, goal "active", activity `crossfit_class`, Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri, 8 weeks, CrossFit box kit, no machines). There is no `.es.md` for this sample, so Spanish was not reviewed.

Measured against: COACH-R4 (R4-SES-02/04/05, R4-PRG-01..04, R4-LIB-01/02, R4-SCP-01/02), CT-01, CX-01..04, R3-VOL-03/04, R2-PER-07, H01-H22, and his own sheet: MASTER MODEL 'EXAMPLE CROSSFIT SPLIT' > 'CLASS PROGRAMMING SPLIT' (MON squat + DUBS, TUES press + snatch, THURS deadlift + gymnastics, FRID clean; HALF TIME table: strength 3x7 to 3x4 then a 1RM, power 3x4 to 3x1; RESISTANCE only L1-2 = 2-3 x 10), as decoded in `analysis/programs/prog_mm_crossfit_tracks.md` §1.4, §1.9, §4.6. Session length was not judged (CT-01).

**Would Lalo hand this to a class member today?** Not quite. The skeleton is his class split. The Olympic lifts follow his HALF TIME ladder exactly, and the main lifts run his 7-6-5-4 rep ladder. The stairway is clean and the progression copy is right. The problem is the resistance layer bolted onto the class. It reads as a physique program: flies, kickbacks, skullcrushers, a Tate press and three curl exercises, with chest, shoulders, triceps and back at 18 sets. Quads are held at 15. On top of that, the optional WOD day carries a chest press that doubles in one week, and the week's change list misreports it. Two engine fixes and a handful of small ones would make this a B+.

## What Lalo would like (praise)

- **His class split, day for day.** Mon squat + double-under skill, Tue press + snatch, Thu deadlift + toes-to-bar skill, Fri clean, Sat WOD class. Squat and deadlift are 3 days apart (H07), and skill sits on the days his sheet names.
- **Olympic dosing is exactly his HALF TIME power ladder.** 3x4, 3x4, 3x3, 3x3, 3x2, 3x2, 3x1, none in week 8 (there is no power level 5). Hang power snatch and clean in block 1, from the floor in block 2. The power copy is his own: "move it fast... no percentages".
- **Main lifts follow his 7-6-5-4 strength ladder**, with a week-1 baseline and a week-8 end test (R2-PER-07). The tested lift goes first and the back-off sets carry the planned RIR plus 1.
- **Order and pairing are right.** Core first and light (2 x 2), power before strength, knee + pull on Monday and Friday, hip + push on Thursday, rows before vertical pulls, incline-first on the push slot of the hip day (R4-LIB-03).
- **The stairway is clean.** 77 to 110, every step +4-9%, groups arrive in week 7 and hold in week 8 (R4-PRG-01). "Same sets: add a little load or reps" is printed in week 8 (CX-02). The capped groups are shown honestly as "15 of 18".
- **Progression is written as a rule the person applies** (R4-PRG-03). "When you hit 20 on every set with 3 left in the tank, add weight and start again at 15." The next-block rule (R4-PRG-04) and the "this is a template you can edit" lines (CX-04) are both there.
- **WODs are real CrossFit pieces** at sensible scaled loads: KB 26-35 lb, DB thruster 25-35 lb, barbell thruster 45-65 lb, medball ≤20 lb (H11). They carry no muscle-set credit (H09) and rotate movements in block 2.
- **The library rules hold.** Farmer carries and burpee broad jumps are offered as optional add-ons dosed by distance (R4-LIB-01/02), every swap prints what it keeps and loses (R4-LIB-04), and each exercise has a YouTube search link with the name to copy (R4-LIB-05).
- **The liability release is at the top and the bottom** (R4-SCP-01/02). Nothing is cut for time and each day only names what is easiest to remove (CT-01, R4-SES-04).
