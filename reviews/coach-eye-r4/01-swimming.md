# Coach-eye review, round 4: sample 01 (swimming, 16-year-old, middle distance, 3 dryland days)

**Grade: B.** It has a few fixes, but the skeleton is his. Lalo would recognise the week: an athletic block first, A day pull + knee, B day push + hip, a third Olympic + strength day, shoulder care every session, no extra cardio, and Olympic lifts paired clean = squat day, snatch = hinge day. He would not hand it over as is, for two reasons. The arm slot of his A/B template is missing on a sport where triceps are a listed priority. And the extra sets go to later and duplicate exercises, plus an optional-day split squat the day after Friday's, while the main lower lifts sit at 3 sets for 12 weeks.

Files reviewed: `samples/plans/01-swimming.en.md`, `samples/plans/01-swimming.json`, `samples/intakes/01-swimming.json`. There is no `.es.md` for this sample. Session length and minutes are not judged (CT-01).

## What he would like

- **Template fidelity.** S1 has the horizontal two-arm pull (Seal Row), squat (Front Squat), vertical pull (Pull-up), lunge, leg extension and calves. S2 has the horizontal press (Flat DB Bench), two-leg hip extension, an incline press in the vertical slot (the incline bias for a swimmer's shoulder, R4-LIB-03), the staggered-stance deadlift and the leg curl. The athletic block comes first every day.
- **Order and pairing are clean.** Core opens every day (light, 2 × 2). Then jumps → ankle hops → throws → Olympic lift → strength. Horizontal comes before vertical on every day. The supersets pair knee with pull and hip with push.
- **Spine days are right.** The heavy squat is Monday and the heavy hinge is Friday. Friday's second knee lift is a non-counted KB front-rack squat.
- **Swimming specifics follow R3-ATH-01.** Shoulder care runs every session with no bands (KB face pulls, prone Y/T, reverse flies). There is no extra cardio, and the plan says the pool is the cardio. Olympic lifts run on 3 days, clean = squat day and snatch = hinge day (R2-ATH-04), dosed 6-8 sets a week, inside the profile's 4.5-7.5. Throws are 36-44 a week, inside 35-45. Medball weights match the balls he owns. There are no sprints and no change-of-direction drills, which is right for a swimmer.
- **Block changes are real one-detail variations.** Examples: alternating staggered deadlift → kickstand deadlift, seated → lying leg curl, single-arm cable row → "1-second squeeze", KB front-rack squat → "pause at the bottom".
- **The stairway is right.** Each group reaches its final sets in week 11 and holds in the test week. No group step exceeds about 11 %, so no 15 % note is needed. Effort drifts from 3 to 2 to 1-2 to 1 + MYO, and the MYO set lands on the leg extension and the lateral raise, never on a compound.
- **Progression is a rule the person applies on every lift.** For example: "when you hit 12 on every set with 3 left in the tank, add weight next time and start again at 10". The next-block rule and the editable-template lines are present (CX-02..04, R4-PRG-03/04).
- **Liability and safety.** The guardian release is at the top and the bottom (R4-SCP-01/02). The plan uses a 3-5RM instead of a max from zero (R2-PER-07). It has the 4-hour gap from hard pool sessions, the taper note and the Christmas-week note. The pre-season block is scheduled to finish before 1 Feb 2027.
- **Swaps and video.** Every swap says in one line what it keeps and what it loses. Every exercise has a YouTube search link and a name to copy (R4-LIB-04/05). The proposed swimmer exercises (external rotation, streamline holds and jumps) are optional add-ons, never template picks (R4-LIB-01).
