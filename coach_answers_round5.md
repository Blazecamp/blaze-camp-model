### R5-VOL Sets and muscle groups

**R5-VOL-01** A high-incline press (the B day's 'Chest / Shoulders' slot): how much does it count for chest?
- Proposal: 1 set for front delts and half a set for chest. A 30-45 degree incline counts a full set for chest.
- Choice: Partly
- Answer (verbatim): "Its mostly upper chest for me like a 1 for chest and a .25 front delts .25 tricep"

**R5-VOL-02** Should every plan keep pulling sets at or above pressing sets?
- Proposal: Yes, every plan. A press may lead the pulls by one set in a week while the back sits on its number.
- Choice: That's right
- Answer (verbatim): "Yes but always be adjustable, a client or coach may modify more focus accordingly"

**R5-VOL-03** Side delts on a physique plan: a minimum of direct sets by level?
- Proposal: At least 4 / 6 / 8 direct sets a week (beginner / intermediate / advanced).
- Choice: That's right
- Answer (verbatim): "Yes but those are like minimum numbers"

**R5-VOL-04** On your A / B days biceps (A) and triceps (B) get one exercise each. Your older answer said 'never one exercise'. OK on A / B days?
- Proposal: Yes. One exercise each on A / B days, as your template has it. Push / pull / legs days keep 2-4 per muscle.
- Choice: That's right
- Answer (verbatim): "Yes But adjustable"

**R5-VOL-05** Which are the 7th and 8th exercises of an A / B day?
- Proposal: A day: calves (7th), rear delts (8th). B day: side delts (7th), calves (8th). As in your BYOP sheets.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-VOL-07** Full-body aesthetics, 4 days: both optional days come right after a press day, and the training days are full at 8 exercises. Chest ends at 23 of its 25 sets. What do we do?
- Proposal: Allow a light chest exercise (fly or machine press) on the optional day even after a press day. Machines and flies are easy on recovery.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-VOL-08** Advanced physique client: Focus muscles start at 22 weekly sets and the rest at 16. Right starting numbers?
- Proposal: Yes: 16 for the others, so the first lift of each day can still climb; Focus muscles start higher.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-VOL-09** An athlete's flat bench press (barbell or DB): at most 4 sets in a session?
- Proposal: Yes. More sets go to the main leg lift first.
- Choice: Partly
- Answer (verbatim): "It can go up more as well depends on focus and goals"

**R5-VOL-10** 2-day pickleball plan: with the new arm slot both days are full, so the calf raise on the B day was dropped (calves 8 to 4 sets) and shoulders end at 7 of 8. Which do you prefer?
- Proposal: Keep the calf raise on both days for racket players 50+ (Achilles care) and let the day hold one more exercise.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-VOL-11** A muscle is 1 set short of its target and an extra exercise would add 2 (football chest: 12 of 13). What do we do?
- Proposal: Give the day's first press a 5th set in the last block, so it reaches 13.
- Choice: That's right

### R5-ATH Athletes

**R5-ATH-01** Athletic A / B days keep a two-leg squat or hinge in slot 2 and a single-leg lift in slot 4, so athletes do less single-leg work than your 80-90 %. OK?
- Proposal: Yes. Keep your template's two-leg and single-leg slots. The extra single-leg work comes from jumps, bounds and the open slots.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-ATH-02** Jumps, throws, cuts and sprint meters: must they go up every week, or may they hold some weeks like sets do?
- Proposal: They may hold for up to 2 weeks, as long as they reach the block's final number.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-ATH-03** Athletes training 2 or 3 days a week: how much of a 4-day week's jumps, throws, cuts and sprints?
- Proposal: About three quarters.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-ATH-04** Incline pressing before overhead pressing is used for swimmers' shoulders. Also for throwers (baseball, softball, javelin) and volleyball?
- Proposal: Yes, same rule for them.
- Choice: That's right
- Answer (verbatim): "Yeah, but going overhead is also important, so I don't want to completely take off overhead pressing or any of that. We bias incline pressing for strength, but overhead pressing should be included for health. It's a variable for health, but it's also important for them to be able to reach up and have that range of motion available."

**R5-ATH-05** A gym session the day before a game: always the lighter one?
- Proposal: Yes.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-ATH-07** 'Stay athletic' players (pickleball, rec leagues): how many exercises a day?

**R5-ATH-08** A 50+ client with a low-back 'hot spot' for their sport but no pain reported: injury-friendly core only (planks, dead bugs, bird dogs)?

**R5-ATH-09** Power lifts for someone with pain, 3+ play days a week, or a 50+ racket player: KB swing or DB/KB high pull instead of snatches and barbell cleans. Is 3 play days the right line?

**R5-ATH-10** Cutting sports (soccer, basketball, tennis...): one single-leg or lateral jump every training day. In the very first weeks (landing on two feet), a two-foot lateral jump instead?

**R5-ATH-11** No hard (Blue) cardio the day before a top-speed sprint day or the week's heavy deadlift day. Right?

**R5-ATH-12** Football B day: Friday's RDL (the week's heavy deadlift) sits at 2 sets for 9 weeks while the DB bench on the same day has 4. How should we fix it?

### R5-CLS Class and bootcamp

**R5-CLS-01** CrossFit-style class: the lunge sits on the clean + pull day so quads train twice a week. Lunge, or a leg press / goblet squat?
- Proposal: Lunge.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-CLS-02** Bootcamp with no pull-up bar: what fills the free station?
- Proposal: A hip thrust or bridge (hips get more work; pressing then leads pulling by one station).
- Choice: That's right

**R5-CLS-03** Circuits of 3-5 rounds: when does the extra round come?
- Proposal: One more round in the last two weeks of the block.
- Choice: Partly
- Answer (verbatim): "You can do one more round accordingly as the person progresses. Mathematically, say it's a 12-week program: 3 rounds the first 4 weeks, then 3 rounds, then 4 rounds the next 4 weeks, then 5 rounds the next 4 weeks, and then people can adjust as they need to."

**R5-CLS-04** Wall balls and sled pushes in the class workout (not only HYROX)?
- Proposal: Yes, with a switch to turn them off.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-CLS-05** A class member's extra lifting: how much?
- Proposal: Arms and shoulders near the 10-set floor plus about 2. Chest and back 14-16. Legs reach their full number. Small muscles at 10-12 reps, not 15-20.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-CLS-06** Bootcamp cardio station: always plain cardio (jump rope, jacks, burpees), or may it be a KB swing or a ball slam?
- Proposal: Always plain cardio by default. A swing or slam is a swap the person can choose.
- Choice: That's right

**R5-CLS-07** Bootcamp: shoulders already start at their top number, and the extra round in the last two weeks takes them past it. OK?
- Proposal: Keep one round count for every station and say it in the plan.
- Choice: Partly
- Answer (verbatim): "Nothing should start at their top number. It should all be gradual, and whatever the focus is, that should be a little bit higher to start. That's it, not complicated."
