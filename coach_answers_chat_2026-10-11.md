# Coach answers in chat, 2026-10-11 (CH5)

Coach Lalo (Gerardo Vera), in the build conversation, answering three questions raised by the engine 0.6 fix round (`engine/DECISIONS.md` G-119, G-130..G-139). Quoted verbatim, then the reading the team applies. Authority: same rank as round 4 on these three subjects only (newest answer).

## CH5-01 Team practices

**Question.** Should team practices count as aerobic sessions for competitive athletes (at most 2 replaced, at least 1 gym session kept)?

**Answer (verbatim):** "team practice doesnt matter, just have the athlete take into account and they handle that"

**Reading.** The plan does not build team practices into the week and does not count them as aerobic sessions. The aerobic sessions and the athletic layer stay as the template and the sport profile set them. The plan prints one line telling the athlete to take their team practices into account and adjust the load themselves. This replaces the PROPOSED "practice counts as aerobic" rule of G-136.

## CH5-02 The arm slot on athletic A / B days

**Question.** Should the arm slot (biceps on A days, triceps on B days) go only to sports whose profile asks for direct arm sets, or to every athletic A / B day?

**Answer (verbatim):** "all should have it and be adjustable always"

**Reading.** Every athletic A / B day holds slot 5 (biceps on the A day, triceps on the B day), for every sport, season and the stay-active plans, as R4-SES-05 lays out. Like any exercise it can be edited or removed by the person (CX-04).

## CH5-03 Big groups above the template's slots

**Question.** On the advanced 4-day physique week, back and chest stop at 20 of their planned 25 weekly sets, because the optional day's bonus exercise is for small muscles only. Plan them to 20, or may big groups use the optional day?

**Answer (verbatim):** "they should reach more if needed, no cap until 35"

**Reading.** A big group reaches its planned final sets: when the main days cannot hold them, it may take a bonus exercise (on the optional day, or on a training day when there is none). No group is capped below its planned final sets by the template. The highest weekly number any group may reach is 35 sets. This replaces the "optional-day bonus for small groups only" part of G-102 for big groups that need it; the bonus still never repeats a pattern trained the day before.
