## 0. Conventions used throughout

### 0.1 The 'dates' are reps per side (OBSERVED)
Many rep cells are stored as dates, for example `datetime(2022, 12, 12)`. Every one of these cells has number format **`m/d`**, so in Google Sheets they display as **'12/12'**, '10/10' or '8/8'. The coach typed `12/12` (12 reps each side) and Sheets auto-converted it to a date.
The proof is PPL novice `B26 = '15/15'` ('DB Rows or Row machine'). It stayed text because month 15 does not exist.
All such cells sit on unilateral or alternating exercises: lunges, single-leg deadlifts, A-drills, book openers, DB rows, single-arm pulldowns and DB overhead press.
**Generator rule:** store reps as text and parse `N/N` as N reps per side.

### 0.2 What counts as a working set
* **'Any Cardio Equipment' 1 × '5 Minutes'** opens every UL and PPL session. It is treated as a warm-up and not counted.
* **'30-40 mi slow/steady CARDIO'** on fat-burn Wednesday and Saturday is a conditioning day and not counted as sets. 'mi' is INFERRED to mean minutes, which matches the UL/PPL note 'max 40 min of steady'.
* **A March / A Skip / A Run** are counted as **athletic sets** (bucket AP_SPRINT) but kept separate from **resistance sets**. 'KB Book openers' counts as a **control set** (CT_CORE_THORAX).
* **All set cells in these three tabs are filled.** There are no data validations or dropdowns, so 'as written' equals 'assumed' everywhere except in two places:
  * **Circuit blocks.** Only the first station of each circuit has a number in SETS (e.g. `B39 = 3`), and the other three stations are blank. INFERRED: that number is the **round count for the whole 4-station circuit**, so each station gets that many sets. As written, the blank stations would have 0 sets.
  * **'MAX REPS' / 'MAX'.** These are counted as sets, with reps = AMRAP. They are excluded from rep totals and mean-rep figures.

### 2.3 Rules visible in UL

1. **Session template (OBSERVED order).** 5-minute general cardio warm-up, then athletic drills (lower day only), then bilateral compounds, then unilateral compounds, then isolation, with calves or arms and delts last.
2. **Lower day sequence (OBSERVED):**
   * Drills: 'A March' then 'A Skip' then 'A Run', an intensity ladder from march to skip to run.
   * Compounds: bilateral knee-dominant ('Sqats or Leg Press'), bilateral hip-dominant ('Deadlifts'), unilateral knee-dominant ('Reverse Lunges'), unilateral hip-dominant ('Single Leg Deadlift').
   * Isolation: quad ('Leg Extensions'), hamstring ('Leg Curls'), calves.
   * Knee and hip dominance **alternate** and **bilateral precedes unilateral**. This is the same order as the BYOP PPL legs template (Bilateral KD, Bilateral HD, Unilateral KD, ...).
3. **Upper day sequence (OBSERVED):** H-pull ('Dumbbell Rows'), H-push ('Bench Press'), V-pull ('Pull ups or Lat pull down'), V-push ('Overhead Press'), biceps, triceps, side raises, upright rows.
   * The order strictly alternates antagonists (pull, push, pull, push, then biceps, triceps), and it **starts with a pull**.
   * INFERRED: natural superset pairs are rows+bench, pulldown+OHP, curls+triceps and side raises+upright rows. No superset label exists in the tab.
4. **Athletic work goes first** on the lower day, right after the general warm-up. There is none on the upper day. (OBSERVED)
5. **Isolation goes last** and is dosed high-rep: 20s or 'MAX REPS'. (OBSERVED)
6. **No core, no carries, no plyometrics beyond A-drills, no conditioning inside sessions.** Steady cardio is a separate recommendation (A5: Wed and Sat, max 40 min). (OBSERVED absence)
7. **Pain rule (A7, OBSERVED).** With joint pain, swap fixed grips (barbells, bars) for split grips (DBs, KBs, individual handles). The first lever is **equipment substitution, not volume reduction.** INFERRED: that is why most compounds carry an 'or' option ('(DB or Barbell)', '(Bodyweight or DBs)', 'Sqats or Leg Press', 'Pull ups or Lat pull down').

### 2.7 What UL reveals (INFERRED)
* **Level is a volume dial, not an exercise dial.** One movement menu, three doses. From NOVICE to NEWBY most rows gain a set (+50% lower-day sets). From NEWBY to INTERMEDIATE the gain is smaller (+7% lower, +12.5% upper) and lower-body reps drop to 8.
* **Two contradictory rep rules sit side by side.** The lower body follows the classic 'more sets, fewer reps, heavier' rule (12, 10, 8), matching A6 'The lower the reps, the higher the weight'. The upper body goes 8, 10, 12 with more sets.
  * Either the upper-day novice was deliberately given fewer, heavier reps for easier technique learning,
  * or the upper block was filled in the opposite direction by mistake.
  * PPL (below) follows the lower-body logic for every compound, which suggests the UL upper day is the anomaly. This is an open question for the coach.
* **Isolation is 'fixed-dose'.** Small-muscle work (20 reps or AMRAP) barely changes with level. Progression lives in the compounds. This matches the coach's 'smaller muscles take a lot more reps'.
* **Even a bodybuilding split carries an athletic primer.** The A-drills appear on every lower day for every level. The coach treats sprint mechanics as basic movement literacy, not as sport-specific work.
* **The weekly distribution is lower-body and shoulder heavy, chest light.** Two side-delt isolations versus one press. This hints at an aesthetic V-taper bias or a shoulder-health bias. An open question for the coach.

### 3.7 What PPL reveals (INFERRED)
* **Novices get high reps (15) and fewer sets; advanced lifters get 8 reps and more sets** on push/pull compounds. This is the canonical 'learn the movement light, then load it' progression, and it states the coach's level logic more clearly than UL does.
* **Delts are the coach's favourite 'filler' muscle.** He slots them between compounds on both upper days: 3 of the 12 push/pull exercises are delt isolation (side raises, reverse flies, upright rows), and there is no overhead press at all.
* **Vertical pushing is optional to him.** OHP is absent from PPL. In fat burn it shows up in only 2 of 8 upper sessions. Side raises cover the 'shoulder' need.
* **The legs day is a reusable module.** One 'lower day' block is dropped unchanged into both UL and PPL. A generator can treat a session as a reusable unit with a level-indexed dose.

### 4.8 What FAT BURN reveals (INFERRED)
* **Fat loss is not a different kind of lifting.** The block is literally titled 'MUSCLE BUILDING'. The fat-loss levers sit around it: two 30-40 min steady cardio days plus two 'recommended' metabolic circuits. The lifting itself is a 4-day hypertrophy upper/lower.
* **The coach thinks in slots with a dose, then rotates exercises inside the slot.** The row (pattern slot) holds the prescription, and variety comes from swapping exercises inside the pattern every week (A/B) and every two weeks (variation upgrade). This is exactly the slot-then-exercise structure a generator needs.
* **Big muscles progress by load (+sets, -reps), small muscles by reps.** Compounds go from 15/12 reps at 3 sets to 8-10 reps at 3-4 sets. Isolation goes from 15-30 reps to 40 reps at 2-4 sets. This matches the coach's statement that smaller muscles 'take a lot more reps because you can't really go that heavy'.
* **Variation upgrades follow a stability/ROM ladder:** machine-supported to free DB row, walking lunge to elevated lunges, flat to incline. Harder versions arrive at the midpoint.
* **Core is a conditioning-circuit item for this population**, not a lifting slot.

**Candidate generator rules** (INFERRED, each traceable to the sections above):
1. **Session order law:** general cardio warm-up (5 min), then prep/athletic drills (lower days), then bilateral compounds, then unilateral compounds, then isolation, with calves or arms last.
2. **Lower-day pattern order:** BKD, BHD, UKD, UHD, ISO_QUAD, ISO_HAM, ISO_CALF. Alternate knee and hip; bilateral before unilateral. FAT BURN may lead with BHD instead (variant B), and then the finisher switches to ISO_HAM.
3. **Upper-day pattern order:** alternate antagonists (pull/push or push/pull) through H then V. One H-push, one V-pull and one H-pull every session. V-push is optional and replaceable by side raises or upright rows. Arms last, biceps and triceps balanced across the week.
4. **Level = dose, not exercise list** (in these tabs). The NOVICE to NEWBY to INTERMEDIATE ladder for compounds:
   * sets 2-3 to 3-4 to 4-5;
   * reps 12-15 to 10 to 8;
   * isolation fixed at 2-3 × 20 or AMRAP.
5. **Week-to-week (4-week block):** net weekly sets +0 / +4 / +6. Compounds gain sets while compound reps drop (15 to 12 to 10 to 8). Isolation reps climb (15 to 20 to 30 to 40) at flat or lower sets. Upgrade exercise variations at the block midpoint (WK3). Never more than 4 sets per slot in the fat-loss template.
6. **Pain substitution:** fixed grip to split grip first (A7).
7. **Cardio:** steady state of at most 40 min on 2 non-lifting days. Circuits are an optional, progressively denser add-on on lifting days.
8. **Store reps as text** ('12/12' = per side, 'MAX REPS' = AMRAP, '30 secs' = time).

**Against the coach's stated model:**
* **'15-20 sets per muscle per week'.** Met for legs in UL (NEWBY+), in PPL at 6 days, and in FAT BURN. Chest, rear delts, calves and abs are systematically under it.
* **'3%, 5% or 7% increments'.** FAT BURN set increments are 0 / 6.7 / 9.4% and rep increments 5.2 / 12.4 / 12.6%. They are close in spirit, but applied as whole sets on individual slots rather than as smooth percentages.
* **'Smaller muscles take more reps'.** True in UL and FAT BURN, where isolation mean reps are about 2-4 times the compound mean. PPL INTERMEDIATE delt accessories at 4 × 8 are the exception.
* **'Everyone picks between 5 and 30 reps'.** The lowest prescribed is 8. The highest is 40 (FAT BURN WK3-4 isolation), outside the stated range.

## 6. Open questions for the coach
