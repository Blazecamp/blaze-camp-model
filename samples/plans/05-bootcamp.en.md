## Before you start

- Week 4 jumps more than usual (33 % more sets in total): adjust if needed.
- Week 7 jumps more than usual (25 % more sets in total): adjust if needed.
- Quads, Chest, Back, Biceps, Triceps: weeks 1-3 run 3 rounds a station. So these groups stay under 10 sets a week until week 4, when the rounds go up. Add a round if you want more sooner.
- Circuit rounds climb with the plan, one more round each third of the block (3 to 5 rounds). These are a guide: add or take away a round as you need.
- Hips (glutes + hamstrings): every station does the same rounds. So this group gets 18.75 sets a week, above its usual top of 18. To stay at the top, keep its stations at 4 rounds when the others do more.
- Optional: pull-up bar or suspension trainer (TRX) would add more exercise choices. The plan already uses what you have.
- With this equipment, the plan has no pull from above (pull-up or pulldown). A row or a rear shoulder fly takes its place. A pull-up bar, a cable or a TRX would add it.
- Your bootcamp mixes two ways to train. Some days you go in rounds through all the stations. Other days you go station by station: all the sets at one station, then the next. Each circuit says which.

## Basic Instruction
> \*Always leave 1 rep in the tank, never work up to failure but get as close as you can.
>
> \*\*Do steady cardio whenever you want, just don't overdo it... I recommend doing it on your aerobic days, max 40 min of steady.
>
> \*\*\*The lower the reps, the higher the weight... Always go for 1-3 RIR (reps in reserve), aka RPE 7+.
>
> \*\*\*\*When there are joint pains, usually going unilateral and/or getting away from fixed grips (barbells, bars) and going to split grips (dumbbells, kettlebells, individual grips) helps take care of some stuff.
>
> \*\*\*\*\*If you have any questions just ask me - IG @BLAZECAMP or TWITTER @BLAZECAMP\_

_This plan starts with 3 reps in the tank. It ends with 1 in the last week. The coach's "1 in the tank" is where you finish. So follow the number for each week._

How hard each week is: weeks 1-4: leave 3 in the tank; weeks 5-6: leave 2 in the tank; week 7: leave 1-2 in the tank; week 8: leave 1 in the tank. Week 8: one extra-hard push on each of these days (Monday, Wednesday and Friday). In the last round, on the marked stations, hold the hardest point for 10 s.

How heavy? Each exercise says how many reps to leave in the tank. "Leave 2 in the tank" means you could still do 2 more reps. That is about an 8 out of 10 for effort. (3 left is a 7. 1 left is a 9.) Your plan starts with 3 left. Then it goes to 2, then 1-2. In the last week it is 1, with a few extra-hard sets. Week 1: start a little lighter. Pick a weight you could lift about 3 more times.

Core supersets are optional. You can pair a core exercise with any exercise to save time.

## How you progress

- Circuits: 3 rounds, then 4 from week 4 and 5 from week 7. The work time at each station goes from 35 s to 50 s, a step every 2 weeks. Add a little weight only if you end a station with more reps left than planned.
- Weeks 4, 7 add more than usual (over about 15 % more sets for a muscle group, or in total). They are marked ↑: adjust if needed.
- You can edit this plan. Change the sets of any exercise on any day or week. Add or take out exercises. Turn blocks off.

## How to adjust this plan

- Missed most of a week? Do that week again before you move on. Missed one day? Do it on the next free day, or let it go. Never squeeze two days into one.
- A set felt like your max, with no rep left? Next time, take one set off that exercise, or a little weight.
- Everything feels easy? First add a little weight or a rep. Never skip ahead more than one week.
- Something hurts? First use a listed swap, then cut that exercise's sets by a third to a half, then drop it for the rest of the block. See a professional if it keeps hurting.
- Life got in the way for a few weeks? Maybe you were sick, away or busy. Stop the block. Take the easy week if you want. Then build a new block.
- You or your coach can turn off these blocks on any day: core and cardio. You can also add or take out exercises, like more rear shoulder work. Use the suggested supersets to save time.
- In a circuit you can skip the jump, throw or cardio station. You can also take out a small-muscle station. The rounds stay the same.
- You or your coach can also change the sets of any exercise, on any day or week. The plan is a starting point you can edit.
- The core block is optional. You may turn it off or change its exercises on any day. You can change anything in this plan.
- Each day shows an average time. It is an estimate, just so you know. Nothing in this plan is cut or changed because of time.
