## Basic Instruction
> \*Always leave 1 rep in the tank, never work up to failure but get as close as you can.
>
> \*\*Do steady cardio whenever you want, just don't overdo it... I recommend doing it on your aerobic days, max 40 min of steady.
>
> \*\*\*The lower the reps, the higher the weight... Always go for 1-3 RIR (reps in reserve), aka RPE 7+.
>
> \*\*\*\*When there are joint pains, usually going unilateral and/or getting away from fixed grips (barbells, bars) and going to split grips (dumbbells, kettlebells, individual grips) helps take care of some stuff.
>
> \*\*\*\*\*If you have any questions just ask me - IG @BLAZECAMP or TWITTER @BLAZECAMP\_

_This plan starts at 3 in the tank and ends at 1 in the last week: the coach's "1 in the tank" is where you finish, so follow each week's number._

Planned effort across the stairway: weeks 1-4: leave 3 in the tank; weeks 5-6: leave 2 in the tank; week 7: leave 1-2 in the tank; week 8: leave 1 in the tank. Week 8: one intensifier on each of these days (Monday, Wednesday and Friday): on the marked circuit stations, hold the hardest point for 10 s at the end of the last round.

How heavy? Each exercise tells you how many reps to leave in the tank: "leave 2 in the tank" means you could do 2 more reps at the end of the set (about RPE 8; 3 left = RPE 7, 1 left = RPE 9). Your plan starts with 3 in the tank, then 2, then 1-2, and in the last week 1 with a few extra-hard sets. Week 1: start a little lighter, with a weight you could lift about 3 more times.

Core supersets are optional anywhere: you can pair a core exercise with any exercise to save time.

## How you progress

- Circuits: 4 rounds, then 5 from week 7; the work time per station goes from 35 s to 50 s, a step every 2 weeks. Add a little weight only when you finish a station with more reps left in the tank than planned.
- Week 7 adds more than usual (over about 15 % more sets for a muscle group, or in total). It is marked ↑: adjust if needed.
- This plan is a template you can edit: change the sets of any exercise on any day or week, add or remove exercises, and switch blocks off.

## How to adjust this plan

- Missed most of a week? Repeat that week before you move on. Missed one session? Do it on the next free day or let it go: never squeeze two sessions into one.
- A set felt like a true max (no rep left)? Next time take one set off that exercise, or a little weight.
- Everything feels easy? Add a little weight or a rep first; never jump ahead more than one week.
- Something hurts? First use a listed swap, then cut that exercise's sets by a third to a half, then drop it for the rest of the block. See a professional if it keeps hurting.
- Life got in the way for several weeks (sick, travel, busy)? Stop the block, take the recommended easy week if you want, then build a new block.
- You (or your coach) can switch off these blocks on any day: core and cardio. You can also add or remove exercises (more rear-delt work, for example). Use the suggested supersets to save time.
- In a circuit you can skip the jump, throw or cardio station, or remove a small-muscle station: the rounds stay the same.
- You (or your coach) can also change the sets of any exercise on any day or week: the plan is a template.
- The core block is optional: you may switch it off or change its exercises on any day. Everything in this plan is adjustable.
- The time shown on each day is an average estimate, for information only: nothing in this plan is cut or changed because of time.
