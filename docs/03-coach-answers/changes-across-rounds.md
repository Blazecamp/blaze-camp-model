# Changes across rounds

The coach answered four questionnaires: 140 questions in round 1, 45 in round 2 (plus two round-1 leftovers), 13 in round 3 and 20 in round 4. Between rounds 3 and 4, on 2026-10-10, he also gave two dated clarifications in conversation: CX-01 to CX-04 on progression and editing, and CT-01 on session time. Later answers sometimes reversed an earlier answer, and often made one more exact. This page lists every such place: 232 links between an earlier and a later answer (31 reversals, 194 refinements, 7 late answers), plus 8 conversation statements that a questionnaire answer changed. The rule is simple: the latest answer wins. Round 4 and CT-01 come first, then CX on its own subjects, then round 3, round 2 and round 1, and any questionnaire answer beats a statement made in conversation.

## How to read this page

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| REVERSED | The earlier answer, or a proposal he had approved, no longer holds. The earlier text is marked "superseded". |
| REFINED | The earlier answer still holds. The later answer made it more exact or added to it. |
| ANSWERED | The question was skipped, not understood or left blank, and was answered in a later round. |
| OPEN | Two answers pull against each other and he has not said which wins. Listed at the end of this page. |
| INFERRED / PROPOSED | The team's reading or design. He has not confirmed it. |

"Before" shows what the earlier answer said, in short. "Now" shows what the later answer set. Where a still later round changed that again, the cell says so. The rule that holds today is always the "Final rule" on the topic page: follow an id to read it.
