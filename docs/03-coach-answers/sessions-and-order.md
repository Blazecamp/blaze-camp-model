# Coach answers: Sessions and order

How one training session and one training week are put together: what comes first, what is paired with what, how many exercises a session holds, how the spine is loaded, when to superset, how much cardio, and how the plan is edited. Core opens the session. Power comes before strength. Rows come before pulldowns. Hip-dominant lifts (deadlifts, hip thrusts) pair with pushes, and knee-dominant lifts (squats, lunges) pair with pulls. A superset is two exercises done back to back. **Since 2026-10-10 a session is sized by its number of exercises, not by minutes** (CT-01, round 4): the form does not ask for a session length, his own A / B and push / pull / legs templates are the skeleton of every day, and nothing is left out automatically. People can switch off a day's athletic or core block, add or remove exercises, and change sets.

This page is part of [the coach answers by topic](index.md). It holds 36 ids: 30 from rounds 1 to 3, the dated clarification CT-01, and 5 from round 4. Every place where a later answer changed an earlier one is also listed in [changes across rounds](changes-across-rounds.md). Read next: [model guide: sessions](../02-model/sessions.md).

## Why, in his words

- "Ideally, for me, I want everybody to start with core work so they can get it out of the way." ([ATH-11](#ath-11))
- "That'll allow me to keep as far away as I can from bleeding over problems when I'm working out." ([UNI-11](#uni-11))
- "I don't want to always load the lower back and the spine twice per week." ([PER-04](#per-04))
- "It's a tool to use to fatigue the heart more and compress the time." ([ORD-05](#ord-05))
- "there's never one exercise, so from 2 all the way to 4" ([R2-SES-05](#r2-ses-05))
- "an athlete or a coach should be able to modify accordingly" ([R3-SES-02](#r3-ses-02))
- "You need to stop trying to be responsible for the people's time." ([CT-01](#ct-01))
- "This is just our recommendation. These are templates that we're giving them for them to add or take away" ([R4-SES-02](#r4-ses-02))
- "Day A: back, quads, and biceps. It's going to be more pull- and knee-dominant movements." ([R4-SES-05](#r4-ses-05))
