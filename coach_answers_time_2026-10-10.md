# Coach clarification: session time is the user's responsibility

Date: 2026-10-10. Source: Lalo Vera, in the Claude Code conversation that built this repository. This is a new answer record, not an edit to any earlier transcript.

**Authority:** `COACH-CT CT-01`. It supersedes earlier answers only on the subject of fitting plans into a chosen session length. All unrelated coach rules still stand.

## CT-01: Do not fit plans to time

The coach's words, verbatim:

> You need to stop trying to be responsible for the people's time. We just need to be able to give them that responsibility and not worry about how long the session is going to be. The logic: we put it out there. The time for the session, they need to be responsible for it. We just worry about the amount of exercises within the session that we've already accounted for and the timing of that session. That's up to the person, the athlete, the coach, the client, whatever that is. We can give an average recommendation of what it should take, but we will never know what it's going to take.
>
> I want you to not get complicated over the time.

**Approved meaning:**
- The plan is sized by the **number of exercises per session**, using the counts the coach already gave (VOL-08, R2-SES-04, VOL-16, PRG-03). It is not sized by minutes.
- The plan may print an **average time estimate** as information. It is never a constraint, and the plan is never cut, reshaped or flagged as failing because of it.
- How long a session actually takes is up to the person, athlete, coach or client.

**What this supersedes:**
- The time-fit parts of R3-SES-02 ("when the time someone picks can't fit your minimums"). Its other parts stand: supersets are still recommended as a way to save time, blocks can still be switched off, and exercises can still be added or removed.
- Every engine and planner rule that treats session minutes as a limit. That covers the time check and time cutters, the 100% / 110% day-length rules, "days over the chosen minutes" as a violation, and planner decisions D1, D2, D3, D12 and D13 in `design/PLANNER_SPEC.md`.
- Session length as a required intake field. Whether to keep it as an optional, informational input is asked in round 4.

**What stays:**
- Exercise-count limits per session.
- The floor of 10 weekly sets on trained groups.
- 2–4 exercises per group per day.
- Every ordering and pairing rule.
- No check-ins, tracking or stored data.

**Open, asked in round 4:** what gives when few training days and the exercise-count limit cannot hold every group at its floor.
